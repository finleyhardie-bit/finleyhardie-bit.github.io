Beyond work

A few things that don't fit on a resume but say a lot about how I work.

Growing up across Europe

I was raised in Malta, Austria, France and Finland before settling in New Orleans. I'm a native French speaker, and moving that often taught me to walk into a new room, figure out how it works and fit in fast. Clients and teammates from different backgrounds feel familiar to me, not foreign.

Soccer: captain and referee

I captained my varsity team at Brother Martin High School to a district championship. I'm also a soccer referee with the Louisiana State Referee Committee, which means making fast calls under pressure and explaining them to people who don't want to hear it.

Leading new members in Sigma Chi

As Executive Chair of New Member Education, I design the program that brings each new class into a chapter of 120+ members. It's part teaching, part planning and part keeping a lot of people on the same page.

Premier League stats nerd

I follow the Premier League closely as a Manchester United fan, which is partly why my database project tracks every club's players. When a hobby turns into a dataset, I'll take it.

Service and discipline

I volunteered 100+ hours serving meals to people in need in high school, and spent two years in the NJROTC Naval Development Program, where I picked up habits I still use: show up early, finish what you start.

Cooking and skiing

Living in Denver means skiing whenever I can. At home I cook; lately that's been steak au poivre and carbonara.
