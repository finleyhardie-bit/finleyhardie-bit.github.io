How I work

I check before I hand off

I tie totals back to the source, audit line by line after a rebuild, and never overwrite an original file. A wrong number in a budget costs more than a slow one.

I make messy data usable

Nine report formats, scanned PDFs, missing months: I write down the rules for handling each case so the next person, or the next month, gets the same answer.

I take feedback and fix it fast

When a principal called a draft "impossible to read," I rebuilt it and it was approved on the second pass.

I respect people's time

I batch my questions into one clear list instead of sending ten emails, and I flag big swings early instead of guessing.

I lead and teach

I run onboarding for a 120+ member organization and have presented technical topics to my class.

I work across cultures

Native French speaker raised across Europe. I'm comfortable with clients and teammates from different backgrounds.
